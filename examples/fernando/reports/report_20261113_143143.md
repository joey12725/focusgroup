## Scoreboard

| Variant | Interviews | Sentiment | Purchase intent | Top objection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $49 once | 4 | Positive | High | "Can I turn the jokes off?" |
| $29 plus $3 a month | 4 | Mixed | Low | The monthly fee itself |

## What Moved People
- **One price, problem solved.** Marcus framed $49 against what he already spends: "less than my third peace lily cost."
- **The voice works on the right people.** Lena: "If the monstera texts 'I am PARCHED, Lena' I'll laugh and actually go water it."
- **Shared households pool money.** Theo would split the cost with roommates, the same way they bought an air fryer.

## What Backfired
- **The subscription poisons gifting.** Renee: "Three dollars a month means I'm gifting someone a bill." Walt said the same about his grandkids.
- **Skeptics anchor on free.** Gordon and Walt compared it to watering on Sundays and a finger in the dirt. Neither would move at any recurring price.
- **Only Maya, the most impulsive buyer in the room, accepted the fee.** She did it on the condition that "the texts stay funny."

## Winner
**$49 once**, medium confidence (8 interviews). Gift buyers and frequent travelers are the strongest pull.
Test next: $39 vs $49 one-time, and a "serious mode" toggle for people who want the alerts without the personality.
