Fernando is a soil sensor about the size of a golf tee. You push it into any pot and
it texts you, in your plant's own voice, when the plant needs water, more light, or
a little encouragement. Each plant gets a personality: a dramatic monstera, a stoic
snake plant, a needy calathea. Setup takes two minutes with the phone app. Battery
lasts about a year. Target: people who own houseplants and keep accidentally
killing them.
